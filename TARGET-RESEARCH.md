## 😤 Pain Points (What They Complain About)

### Source: @chrisgirbu viral thread (41.6K views, May 18 2026)

> "Content Rewards is lowkey a scam. Here's what actually happens when you run a campaign on there:
> You deposit your budget. You set a CPM rate. And then hundreds of random kids (most of them have 0 clue how to actually clip) start downloading your content, running it through some cheap AI tool to create the clips. That's what you're paying $10K for.
> The clippers don't know your niche. Yesterday they were posting ishowspeed shorts.
> You don't own a single one of those accounts. Every page that posts your content belongs to a random clipper who'll move on to the next campaign the second yours runs dry.
> The moment your budget hits zero, the entire distribution network disappears. No accounts to keep. **No data to learn from. No infrastructure you can build on.**"

**Pain points extracted:**
1. ❌ Bot/AI-generated clips — "running it through some cheap AI tool"
2. ❌ Irrelevant creators — "they don't know your niche"
3. ❌ No account ownership — "you don't own a single one of those accounts"
4. ❌ Zero data/insights — "No data to learn from"
5. ❌ No infrastructure — "No infrastructure you can build on"
6. ❌ Budget waste — "That's what you're paying $10K for"

### Source: Forum/industry complaints

| Pain point | Source | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow approvals** | BlackHatWorld forum | "campaigns take days to approve your clip" |
| **No review SLA** | ClipAffiliates | "campaign owners can leave submissions unreviewed indefinitely" |
| **Budget burns too fast** | Multiple guides | "campaign reward pools running out before a clipper's views finish verifying" |
| **Can't benchmark CPM** | Apify scrapers exist | Campaign owners want to compare rates — scrapers were built because Whop doesn't show this |
| **Can't track ROI** | Payoff Group article | "relate Whop Content Rewards to D2C sales with GA4, UTMs, cohorts" — they're building this manually |
| **Bot views** | FindClout complaints article | "$1,500 SaaS campaign... estimated was almost entirely bot views" |
| **Fake engagement** | Multiple sources | "payout math clustering suspiciously at the exact cap" |

### Source: Existing tools (proves demand)

| Tool | What it does | What it's missing |
|---|---|---|
| **Apify Content Rewards Scrapers** (3 different ones) | Scrape campaign directory for CPM rates, budgets, burn rates | No analytics, no ROI, no creator tracking |
| **autoclip.dev** | Tracks clipper earnings | Creator-focused, not campaign-owner focused |
| **findclout.com** | Tracks Content Rewards complaints and rates | Content/blog, not a dashboard |
| **Payoff Group** | Consulting for CR → e-commerce ROI | Manual consulting, not self-serve tool |

**Key insight:** People are building scrapers and manual processes because **no one has built the dashboard yet.** That's ClipStack.
